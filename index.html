Chapter III

From Belly's Handwritten Notebook

Marginalia, golden kitchen secrets, and handwritten wisdom gathered across decades of family meals.

May 12, 1991

Secret to Flaky Crust

"Never work dough with warm hands. Keep ice water nearby and grate the butter frozen into the flour. Touch it as little as possible — love needs lightness here."

— Belly
Autumn Harvest

The Sofrito Foundation

"Onions, celery, and carrots must be chopped uniformly small and simmered on low flame with olive oil for 25 minutes. Do not rush the gold!"

— Belly
Sunday Evening

Sourdough Whispers

"Listen to the crust when it comes out of the Dutch oven. If it crackles and sings, you’ve achieved perfect moisture balance inside."

— Belly
Summer Notes

Salting Tomatoes

"Always slice heirloom tomatoes onto linen towels and dust with sea salt 20 minutes before assembling a tart. It draws out liquid so the crust stays crisp."

— Belly

Volcanic Garden Soil

A Day in Belly's Kitchen

Follow the rhythmic flow of a traditional day spent around the wooden cutting board.

07:00 AM
☕

Morning Sourdough Feed

Stirring warm water and stone-ground flour into the 12-year-old sourdough starter while the morning kettle boils.

10:30 AM
🧺

Garden Harvest & Herbs

Walking through the dew-dusted garden to clip fresh thyme, sage sprigs, and ripe vine tomatoes into the wicker basket.

01:00 PM
🍝

Rolling Pasta Ribbon Dough

Dusting the wooden table with flour, creating a well for farm-fresh egg yolks, and rolling ribbons of golden pappardelle.

04:30 PM
🍲

Slow Simmering the Ragù

The Dutch oven bubbles gently on the back burner. Aromas of red wine, crushed garlic, and bay leaves fill every room.

07:30 PM
🕯️

Lighting Candles & Tour de Table

Placing warm galettes and crusty bread onto linen tablecloths. Wine glasses clink as family and friends settle in.
